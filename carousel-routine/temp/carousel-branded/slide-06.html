The New Playbook
3 shifts winning staffing firms are making
1
Replace resume screening with live problem solving. Present a real client scenario and evaluate the candidate's thinking process in real time.
2
Verify through work trials, not interviews. A two hour paid project reveals more than six rounds of behavioral questions.
3
Build placement longevity guarantees. Track retention at six and twelve months. A placement that sticks for two years is worth more than three that back out in six months.
